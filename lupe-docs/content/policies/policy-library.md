## Principles that already apply

- You keep full ownership of your store, datasets, pixels and ad accounts.
- Lupe is a dashboard layer. Processing happens in accounts that you own (GTM, Shopify, ad platforms, Stape).
- Lupe and TDS never store or view your data, and we don't read or cache personal data through the platforms' APIs.
- You can remove our access at any time. See [Your data and our access](page:your-data-our-access).
