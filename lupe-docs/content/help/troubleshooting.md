## I see an error in Meta Business settings or TikTok Business Center

Meta and TikTok have to work across a huge range of devices, operating system versions and browsers, so glitches are common and errors sometimes appear. Try clearing your cache or using a different browser. If something still isn't working, our onboarding team will resolve it with you.

## My Shopify collaborator code isn't working

Check you're logged into the Shopify account that owns the store. Being signed into a different account is the most common cause. You can also generate a new code under :ui[Settings › Users › Security].

## The dataset or pixel says it's owned by another business

It has only been shared with you, for example by a record label, agency or partner, so you can't give us access to it.

- **Meta:** create your own dataset. See [Need to create a new dataset?](page:meta-dataset-access) in the Meta guide.
- **TikTok:** if your pixel only lives inside an ad account, ask us and we'll help you move it on a short call.

## I'm an admin but can't see the pixel or dataset

Being an admin doesn't give you every asset automatically.

- **TikTok:** go to **Users**, click **View** next to your name and open **Assigned assets**. If your pixel isn't listed, click **Add accounts and assets** and give yourself access.
- **Meta:** if you're new to Meta Business Manager, ad accounts and datasets must be assigned to you as a user before you can access them.

## My Meta dataset isn't linked to my ad account

Open the dataset's **Connected assets** tab. If the ad account you advertise from isn't listed, assign it, or ask a member of the Lupe team to do it with you on a short call.

## We need to change the Shopify permissions

Shopify doesn't let collaborator permissions be edited. If we ever need something different, we'll ask you to remove our access and approve a fresh request.
