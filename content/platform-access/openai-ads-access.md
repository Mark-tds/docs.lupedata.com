If you advertise in ChatGPT, we can connect your server-side container to OpenAI's pixel and Conversions API, so purchases, leads and sign-ups from ChatGPT ads are measured with the same consent checks as every other platform. OpenAI Ads Manager adds people by email, so you invite our analytics address to one advertising account.

:::info Ads Manager is new
OpenAI's Ads Manager is still rolling out, and menus can change. If you buy ChatGPT ads through an agency, ask them to follow these steps or contact your onboarding manager.
:::

## Your steps

:::steps
:::step title="Open your advertising account" label="Your part"
In OpenAI's Admin Console, select your organisation, then the advertising account you use for ChatGPT ads.
:::
:::step title="Invite us" label="Your part"
Open **Users & groups** and click **Invite**. Enter our email address:

:::copy value="analytics@lupedata.com" label="Lupe | TDS" caption="Email address to invite"
:::

Give us the **Member** role on this advertising account only. A role is set per advertising account, so you don't need to add us anywhere else.
:::
:::step title="Let us know you're done" label="Your part"
Send your onboarding manager the advertising account name and your **pixel ID**, which you'll find in the advertising account. If OpenAI has given you a Conversions API key, don't email it. We'll set it up with you securely.
:::
:::

## What this access does, and doesn't, give us

| Area | Access | What that means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your chosen advertising account | :yes[Member] | Check your pixel and conversion events, and connect the Conversions API. |
| Account administration | :no[No] | Members can't manage users or account settings. |
| Other advertising accounts | :no[No] | Roles apply to one advertising account at a time. |
| ChatGPT workspaces | :no[No] | Ads access is separate from any ChatGPT or API workspace. |
| Campaigns and budgets | :no[Not used] | We don't change campaigns, bids or budgets unless you ask. |

## Good to know

:::check You're always in control
You can change or remove our access at any time from **Users & groups** in your advertising account.
:::
