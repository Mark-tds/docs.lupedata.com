You only need the guides for platforms your business uses. Each one is done from your own account, shares the minimum we need, and can be removed by you at any time.

## Ad platforms

| Platform | How we're added | What you share | Time |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Meta](page:meta-dataset-access) | Partner business "Lupe \| TDS" | One dataset | About 5 minutes |
| [TikTok](page:tiktok-pixel-access) | Partner Business Center "Lupe \| TDS" | One pixel | About 5 minutes |
| [Google Ads](page:google-ads-access) | Link to our manager account "Lupe \| TDS Manager Account" | Your Google Ads account | About 5 minutes |
| [LinkedIn](page:linkedin-access) | Partner in LinkedIn Business Manager | One ad account | About 5 minutes |
| [Pinterest](page:pinterest-access) | Partner in Pinterest Business Manager | One ad account | About 5 minutes |
| [ChatGPT ads (OpenAI)](page:openai-ads-access) | Invite analytics@lupedata.com | One advertising account | About 5 minutes |
