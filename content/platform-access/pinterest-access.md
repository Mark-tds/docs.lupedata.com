To set up the Pinterest tag and the Pinterest Conversions API, we need access to one ad account. You share it with us as a partner from your own Business Manager, nothing else is shared, and you can remove our access at any time.

:::cards cols="2"
:::card title="Your part" icon="users" tone="mint"
- Add us as a partner
- Share one ad account with us
- Let us know you're done
:::
:::card title="Our part" icon="flow" tone="violet"
- Check your Pinterest tag and events
- Connect your server-side container (Conversions API)
- Check deduplication and consent signals
- Confirm with you when tracking is live
:::
:::

## Before you start

- You'll need **admin** access to your Pinterest business account and Business Manager.
- Ask your onboarding manager for our **Pinterest business ID**.

## Your steps

:::steps
:::step title="Open Business Manager" label="Your part"
Log in to your Pinterest business account. Click the menu icon in the top-left corner, then under **Manage business** select **Business Manager**.
:::
:::step title="Add us as a partner" label="Your part"
Click **Partners** in the left-hand menu, then **Add partner**. Enter the business ID your onboarding manager sent you and select **Invite a partner to access your assets**.

:::warning Add us as a partner, not an employee
Pinterest treats employees and partners differently. Adding us as an employee can stop ad account access working.
:::
:::
:::step title="Share your ad account" label="Your part"
Click **Show assets**. On the **Accounts** tab, find the ad account your business advertises from and switch on **Analyst** only. Leave the **Profile** tab unticked. Click **Next**, check the summary, then click **Send invite**.
:::
:::step title="Let us know you're done" label="Your part"
Send your onboarding manager the **name and ID** of the ad account you shared. We'll accept the invite from our side.
:::
:::

## What this access does, and doesn't, give us

| Area | Access | What that means |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your chosen ad account | :yes[Analyst] | View and manage conversion tags, upload conversions, and view reporting. |
| Campaigns and ads | :no[No] | No creating or editing campaigns, ad groups or ads. |
| Billing and invoices | :no[No] | No payment methods, invoices or business settings. |
| Profiles and boards | :no[No] | No publishing to your Pinterest profile. |
| Catalogs and audiences | :no[No] | No changes to product catalogs or audiences. |

:::note If we need more
Generating a Conversions API access token can need a higher permission on some accounts. If so, we'll ask you to adjust the role or generate the token with you on a short call.
:::

## Good to know

:::check You're always in control
You can change or remove our access at any time from **Partners** in your Business Manager.
:::
