Lupe is a Transparent Digital Services (TDS) company, and the TDS team sets up and supports your store.

Whether you sell merch as an artist or run an ecommerce brand, the Shopify setup is the same. Your store sends consented events to a server on your own domain, and that server passes them to your ad and analytics platforms through their official server connections. Your browser pixels stop being the only thing your ad platforms learn from.

## What we set up in your store

:::cards cols="2"
:::card title="Stape Conversion Tracking app" icon="store"
You install it, we configure it. It connects your store to your own server-side Google Tag Manager container.
:::
:::card title="Checkout events" icon="flow"
Shopify customer events send checkout and purchase data to your server container, so sales aren't lost when browser pixels are blocked.
:::
:::card title="Your consent banner" icon="shield"
Depending on your consent platform (CMP), we replace Shopify's default banner and check consent signals reach the server.
:::
:::card title="A cleaner theme" icon="check-circle"
We find old Universal Analytics tags, pixels firing without consent signals and conflicting scripts, so you know what's running.
:::
:::

## Your three steps

Do these in order. Each one is done from your own account, and you can remove our access at any time.

:::steps
:::step title="Shopify access & Stape setup" label="About 10 minutes"
Send us your collaborator request code, approve our request and install the Stape app. [Open the Shopify guide](page:shopify-stape-setup).
:::
:::step title="Meta dataset access" label="About 5 minutes"
Assign us as a partner on the one dataset your store uses. [Open the Meta guide](page:meta-dataset-access).
:::
:::step title="TikTok pixel access" label="About 5 minutes"
Add us as a partner and share the one pixel your store uses. [Open the TikTok guide](page:tiktok-pixel-access).
:::
:::

## What we never touch

We don't ask for access to your orders, products, customer lists, payments or staff accounts, and we never ask for passwords. See [Your data and our access](page:your-data-our-access) for every permission and why we need it.
