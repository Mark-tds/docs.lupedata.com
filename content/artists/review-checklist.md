Legal frameworks and data systems are evolving and changing rapidly. This checklist helps teams collaborate to update and modernise their system together.

For each step, pick whichever fits best and add a short note: the reason, the rule or clause behind it, and any suggested change. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go. When you're finished, click **Copy summary** and paste it into an email to [mark@transparentdigitalservices.com](mailto:mark@transparentdigitalservices.com).

:::note
Decisions on legal compliance rest with the artist, label and their legal advisers.
:::
