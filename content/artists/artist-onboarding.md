The setup is built around accounts the artist (or the artist's company) owns: the website, the server endpoint on the artist's domain, and the ad accounts. Before you start, it helps to know who owns what.

## Check who owns your accounts

:::warning Pixels and datasets shared by a label can't be passed on
If your Meta dataset or TikTok pixel is owned by a record label, agency or partner, it has only been shared with you, and you can't give us access to it. You can create your own dataset or pixel, or ask the owner to share it with us directly. We're happy to help on a short call.
:::

Who acts as controller of fan data, and who owns the website, data and ad accounts, is usually set out in the artist's recording, licensing or management agreements. See [Who does what](page:who-does-what) for how the roles usually work.

## Your access guides

:::steps
:::step title="Your website or merch store" label="Under 5 minutes"
If your site or merch store runs on Shopify, approve our collaborator request and install the Stape app: :logo[shopify][Shopify guide](page:shopify-stape-setup). If it runs on WordPress, install the Stape plugin: :logo[wordpress][WordPress guide](page:wordpress-stape-setup).
:::
:::step title="Meta dataset" label="Under 3 minutes"
Assign us as a partner on the one dataset the artist's website uses, and check it's linked to the ad account you advertise from. [Open the Meta guide](page:meta-dataset-access).
:::
:::step title="TikTok pixel" label="Under 3 minutes"
Add us as a partner and share the one pixel the artist's website uses. [Open the TikTok guide](page:tiktok-pixel-access).
:::
:::step title="Let your onboarding manager know" label="Under 1 minute"
Send the names and IDs of the dataset and pixel you shared. The platforms don't always notify us, so this is how we know to start.
:::
:::

## Bringing your label or legal team in

If your label's legal team has questions, share the [Shared guide for labels and legal teams](page:artist-guide). It walks through the data flow one step at a time, and ends with a [review checklist](page:review-checklist) where they can say which step a concern relates to, and why.
