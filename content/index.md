## Platform access guides

Each guide takes about 5 to 10 minutes. You complete a few steps in your own account, then let your onboarding manager know you're done.

:::cards cols="3"
:::card title="Shopify access & Stape setup" href="page:shopify-stape-setup" icon="store"
Approve limited collaborator access and install the Stape Conversion Tracking app. About 10 minutes.
:::
:::card title="Meta dataset access" href="page:meta-dataset-access" icon="database"
Assign us as a partner on one dataset in your Meta Business portfolio. About 5 minutes.
:::
:::card title="TikTok pixel access" href="page:tiktok-pixel-access" icon="pixel"
Add us as a partner and share one pixel from your TikTok Business Center. About 5 minutes.
:::
:::

## How we work with your accounts

:::cards cols="3"
:::card title="You own everything" icon="lock" tone="violet"
Lupe and TDS never own or control your datasets or ad accounts, and never store or view your data.
:::
:::card title="Minimum access" icon="shield" tone="violet"
We only ask for the one pixel, dataset or permission each setup needs. Everything else stays private.
:::
:::card title="Clear terms" icon="file" tone="violet"
Read our [Privacy policy](page:privacy-policy), [Terms of service](page:terms-of-service) and [Data processing agreement](page:data-processing-agreement).
:::
:::

Contact your onboarding manager or email [contact@lupedata.com](mailto:contact@lupedata.com). If something in a guide doesn't match what you see on screen, let us know and we'll walk through it with you on a short call.
