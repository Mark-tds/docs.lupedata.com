Use this checklist with your team, your legal adviser or your data protection lead. For each step, pick whichever fits best and add a short note: the reason, the rule or clause behind it, and any suggested change. Your answers are saved in this browser as you go. When you're finished, click **Copy summary** and paste it into an email to [contact@lupedata.com](mailto:contact@lupedata.com).

:::note
Decisions on legal compliance rest with your business and your legal advisers. See [How your data flows](page:how-your-data-flows) for the detail behind each step.
:::
