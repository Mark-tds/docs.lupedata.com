Lupe is a Transparent Digital Services (TDS) company. TDS sets up and maintains your first-party data infrastructure, and the Lupe dashboard keeps watch over it.

Most businesses already pay for the tools that bring customers in: ads, analytics, email, a booking or checkout system. The question for an owner is simple. Is every pound of that spend learning from accurate data, and is every customer's choice about tracking being respected?

For most of the sites we look at, the honest answer to both is "not quite". Lupe fixes that at the source.

## How it works, in one line

Customers choose their consent preferences on your site. With consent, events go to **data.yourbusiness.com**, a server on your own domain. That server checks consent again, removes or hashes anything a platform doesn't need, and sends events to your own ad and analytics accounts through their official server connections.

## What it means for the business

| | Before | With Lupe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ad spend | Platforms learn from what browsers let through | Platforms learn from complete, consented signals |
| Reporting | Every tool counts sales differently | One source of truth for every platform |
| Consent | Depends on every app and tag being set up correctly | Checked in the browser, then again on the server |
| Ownership | Data and audiences spread across apps and suppliers | Your domain, your server, your ad accounts |
| Breakage | Found weeks later, in a bad month | Lupe alerts you when data stops flowing |
