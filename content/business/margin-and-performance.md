Ad platforms decide who sees your ads, and how much you pay to reach them, based on the conversions they can see. When they see only part of your sales, they optimise toward the wrong people and you pay more for each customer. Better data doesn't change your budget. It changes how hard your budget works.

## Where margin leaks

:::cards cols="2"
:::card title="Hidden conversions" icon="pixel"
Ad blockers and browser privacy features stop many browser pixels from firing. Sales still happen, but the platform never learns from them.
:::
:::card title="Broken or doubled events" icon="warning"
Old apps, theme changes and agency scripts send events twice, to the wrong dataset, or not at all. Reporting stops matching your bank balance.
:::
:::card title="Slow pages" icon="clock"
Every third-party script adds weight. Slower pages, especially on mobile, mean fewer people reach checkout.
:::
:::card title="Rented audiences" icon="users"
When data sits in suppliers' accounts, you rebuild audiences every time you change agency or app.
:::
:::

## How first-party data helps

- **Complete, consented signal.** Events go server to server from your own domain, so far fewer are lost to blockers. Platforms optimise on what actually sells.
- **Better match quality.** Your server sends hashed contact details that customers entered, so platforms can match more conversions to the right people.
- **One source of truth.** Every platform receives the same, correctly formatted events, so you can compare results across channels.
- **Fewer scripts in the browser.** Vendor pixels move off the page, which keeps the site lighter.
- **Breakage caught early.** Lupe alerts you when data stops flowing, before a bad month shows up in your numbers.

## What to watch after go-live

| Measure | What it tells you |
| --- | --- |
| Event match quality (Meta) | How well your server events match to real people. Higher is better. |
| Server events received | Whether conversions are reaching each platform reliably. |
| Cost per acquisition (CPA) | What you pay for each new customer. |
| Return on ad spend (ROAS) | Revenue earned for each pound spent on ads. |
| Data recovered in Lupe | Events that ad blockers and browsers would previously have thrown away. |

Actual performance gains vary by campaign, audience and consent rate. We're happy to share results from comparable setups on request.
