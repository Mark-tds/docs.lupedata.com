You keep ownership of every account throughout. You grant access from your own accounts, and you can remove it at any time.

## From first call to go-live

:::steps
:::step title="We look at your site together" label="Step 1"
We run your site through our compliance scanner to see what happens on first load, when a visitor accepts and when they reject, and which scripts and pixels are running. You get a clear picture of where data and consent are breaking today.
:::
:::step title="You choose your consent platform" label="Step 2"
By default we use CookieYes as the consent management platform (CMP). Other Google-certified CMPs can be used at your request. The CMP account is yours, under your own terms with the provider.
:::
:::step title="You grant access from your own accounts" label="Your part · about 20 minutes"
Complete the guides that apply to your business:

- [Shopify access & Stape setup](page:shopify-stape-setup), if you sell through Shopify
- [Meta dataset access](page:meta-dataset-access)
- [TikTok pixel access](page:tiktok-pixel-access)

Then let your onboarding manager know you're done.
:::
:::step title="We build and test your first-party setup" label="Our part"
We set up your server-side Google Tag Manager container on Stape, in an account you own, ideally in a UK or EU region. We connect it to your ad platforms through their server connections, check consent signals, advanced matching and deduplication, and run test events.
:::
:::step title="Go live, then Lupe keeps watch" label="Step 5"
We confirm with you when tracking is live. From then on, the Lupe dashboard alerts you when data stops flowing or consent signals fail, for example after a site update.
:::
:::

## What you'll need

- Admin access to your website platform, your Meta Business portfolio and your TikTok Business Center.
- To know which pixel and dataset your website uses, and that your business owns them.
- If your legal or IT team approves third-party apps, sign-off for **Stape Conversion Tracking** (Shopify stores) before you install it.

:::note What stays your responsibility
Your business decides which platforms receive data, and is responsible for your consent banner wording and privacy notice. See [Compliance essentials](page:compliance-essentials).
:::
