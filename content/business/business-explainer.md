<p class="ch-label">01 · Why you're here</p>
<h2 class="ch-title">You already know your data matters. <em>Now it has to pay off.</em></h2>
<p>Every channel you pay to grow through learns from your data. When that data is incomplete, part of your spend is <strong>working blind</strong>, and that comes straight out of your margin.</p>
<div class="ch-grid">
<div class="ch-card"><b>Online sales</b><span>Product views, baskets and checkouts. Every order should teach the next campaign who to reach.</span></div>
<div class="ch-card"><b>Bookings and leads</b><span>Tables, rooms, appointments and enquiries. Know which campaigns fill your diary.</span></div>
<div class="ch-card"><b>Repeat customers</b><span>Email, loyalty and remarketing. Bring people back without paying to find them twice.</span></div>
</div>
</section>
<section class="ch" id="problem">
<p class="ch-label">02 · Where most businesses are</p>
<h2 class="ch-title">Your customer data is everywhere. <em>Except with you.</em></h2>
<ul class="ch-issues">
<li><div><b>The customer relationship often isn't yours</b><span>Audiences and data sit in apps, plugins and agency accounts you don't control.</span></div></li>
<li><div><b>Spend is often wasted or inefficient</b><span>Ad blockers and browser privacy features hide a large share of conversions before any platform sees them.</span></div></li>
<li><div><b>Too many moving parts</b><span>Scripts added over the years by different suppliers slow your site and each count sales differently.</span></div></li>
<li><div><b>Consent may be broken, silently</b><span>A tag fires after a customer says no, and nobody knows until it becomes a complaint.</span></div></li>
</ul>

<section class="ch" id="stakes">
<p class="ch-label">03 · Why it can't wait</p>
<h2 class="ch-title">The rules are converging. <em>When consent breaks, it costs you.</em></h2>
<p>Different laws, one direction: a clear consent choice for the customer, correctly formatted data for your marketing tools, and a clear record of who handles it. In the US, class actions under laws like California's CIPA and the VPPA target sites whose pixels send data without a valid choice. In the UK and EU, regulators fine.</p>
